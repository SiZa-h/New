# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Respecting the privacy and boundaries of other participants.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without consent.

## Reporting

For behavior that needs attention, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/SiZa-h). Do not post private or sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports and take action appropriate to the circumstances, including removing content or restricting participation. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

# 行为准则

我们希望所有人都能在尊重、友善的环境中参与本项目。

我们期待参与者：

- 体谅他人，以尊重和建设性的方式交流，并尊重不同观点。
- 专业地提出和接受反馈。
- 针对观点与工作提出批评，而不是攻击个人。
- 尊重他人的隐私与界限。

骚扰、歧视、威胁、人身攻击，以及未经同意泄露他人隐私，均不可接受。

## 举报

如需举报，请通过[维护者的 GitHub 个人主页](https://github.com/SiZa-h)提供的联系方式私下联系维护者。请勿在公开 issue 中发布私人或敏感信息。维护者会审查报告，并根据情况采取适当措施，包括移除内容或限制参与。不得报复善意提出问题的人。
